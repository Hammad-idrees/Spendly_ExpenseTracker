Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the data you enter directly — expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with account activity like login timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate and improve the Service, including to:

  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Record, categorize, and display your expenses
  • Generate spending summaries, trends, and reports within the app
  • Communicate important updates about your account or the Service

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Once deleted, this information cannot be recovered, except where we are required to retain certain records by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party providers for functionality such as hosting, authentication, or analytics. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out to us through the contact details provided in your account settings.
